Method

Themes & sources

{{ profile.project.tagline }}. Every week an automated sweep collects metadata from the sources below, applies a keyword relevance gate, and has a language model score and annotate the survivors. A member of the team then reviews the list before anything is published. The sweep profile is maintained by the team and changes as research focuses shift.

How items are chosen

  1. Harvest. Open scholarly metadata (OpenAlex), global news indexes (GDELT), and publisher and institutional RSS feeds. Only titles, links, dates and abstracts are collected.
  2. Gate. An item must mention a finance term and either a case-study city/institution or at least two theme terms. Routine market chatter is filtered out by negative keywords.
  3. Annotate. Claude rates relevance (0–3) against the project brief, assigns cities and themes, and drafts a one-line note.
  4. Review. A team member ticks what to publish and features the most important items.
  5. From the archive. Older works (10+ years) cited by this week's new research are surfaced through the citation graph.
